▪ Do I need to pay a tuition fee to study at the program?

  There is no tuition fee. However, students have to pay a regular administrative
  term fee (Semestergebühren) which includes a 6-month ticket for all public
  transportation in the state of Brandenburg and in Berlin. More details on those
  fees can be found here:

  https://www.uni-potsdam.de/en/studium/studying/organizing-your-studies/re-
  registration.html
  Further information regarding the Semesterticket can be found on the webpage of
  the General Students Committee (Allgemeiner Studierendenausschuss, AStA):

  https://astaup.de/service/semesterticket/

▪ Is MSc Cognitive Science an NC (“Numerus Clausus”) admission restriction
  program? How many places are available each year?

  Yes, the program has restricted admission, meaning that there are only 20 first
  semester study places to be filled annually.

▪ Which career paths does the MSc Cognitive Science program prepare
  graduates for?

  The program provides graduates with a solid expertise in the field of Cognitive
  Science and good skills in doing research. The graduates with research aspirations
  may consider following the research path through a PhD (at the UP or other
  Universities), other graduates can find employment outside Academia – in start-
  ups, tech companies with a cognitive slant, government panels, etc.

▪ What are the main admission requirements?

  For a successful application you need to provide:
  (1) a BA degree that is related to Cognitive Science
  (2) proofs of either your mathematical training (linear algebra, calculus) and/ or
  practical training in experimental psychology (designing, conducting, analyzing,
  presenting an experiment) and/ or skills in programming experiments – you need
  at least 6 ECTS in two of these three domains
  (3) a certificate of your English language skills – C1 level

  To enter the program, German language skills need not be demonstrated.

  For the complete list of required documents please see (+ the subsection
  “Cognitive Science”):

  https://www.uni-potsdam.de/en/studium/application-enrollment/application-
  master/consecutive.html

▪ Which degrees are considered as related to Cognitive Sciences?

  The degrees in General Psychology, Cognitive Psychology, Cognitive
  Neuroscience, or Cognitive Science as well as degrees in Philosophy, Linguistics,
  Robotics and AI with a clear link to cognitive processes are considered as relevant
  and qualifying for the program.

▪ My BA is not closely related to Cognitive Sciences. Can I still apply?

  Most of the regular applicants have a BA in Psychology/Neuroscience/Cognitive
  Sciences, but the program values and encourages diversity of backgrounds, since
  Cognitive Science can be studied from different viewpoints. Therefore, the
  degrees in Neurobiology, Human Biology, Physical Exercise/Sports, Medicine,
  and Computer Science with strong cognitive focus can be also accepted as
  qualifying if applicants - holders of these BAs satisfy all admission
  requirements.

  On the contrary, students with a standard engineering background or with interest
  in clinical issues will be disappointed by the program and, therefore, should not
  apply.

▪ I am currently enrolled in a MA/MSc program, or I have already completed
  an MA/MSc – can I still apply?
  Prospective students who were or are enrolled in a master's program that is the
  same or related to the subject of the current program, or if it is in a similar
  academic field, must apply for and obtain from the Study Board a placement
  notice prior to submitting an application and enclose the notice in the application.
  Only candidates who obtained a placement notice for the first semester entry can
  take part in the first semester application process. The placement procedure is
  meant to guarantee that prospective students who qualify for advanced semester
  entry based on academic credentials obtained in previous studies do not prevent
  someone qualified for first semester entry to receive an admission offer - that is
  important since the places in the program are limited to 20.
  In case the Study Board places you in the higher semester, you can submit an
  application for it (please note that the deadlines for this application are different
  from the first semester application). The credit points obtained in similar courses
  in your previous MSc degree will then be transferred.
  However, if the similarity between two Master degrees is very high, the Board
  may deny placement in the MSc program and suggest applying for a PhD.


  So, as a holder of the MSc degree in a closely related field, you first need to check
  for the overlaps between the courses in your MSc program with the MSc
  Cognitive Science (the course list is available on the program's webpage) and if
  you have obtained at least 15 ECTS in similar courses, it would qualify you for
  an advanced semester entry. Then you will need to follow a placement procedure
  and apply for a higher semester.


  Important: If the placement notification is missing, you will be excluded from
  the admission procedure.
  You can find complete information about the placement notification and the link
  to the form in the FAQ “When do I need placement notification?” here:
  https://www.uni-potsdam.de/en/studium/application-enrollment/application-

▪ Can you provide more information on the requirement of Experimental
  Psychology Practice, Mathematics skills and Experimental Programming
  skills ?

  Since the program has a strong research focus, it is obligatory for all applicants to
  have experience in either Experimental Psychology Practice and/ or Mathematics
  and or Experimental Programming (equivalent to 6 ECTS in two of these three
  domains) prior to the application. This requirement can pose a serious problem
  and be an exclusion criterion for students with some BAs (e.g. Philosophy) that
  usually lack all three courses in the curricula.

  In this case, to cover this requirement applicants - holders of such degrees prior
  to their application need either to take additional courses in Mathematics and/ or
  Experimental Programming and/ or to arrange an internship in the lab that
  conducts Psychology/ Neuroscience/ Cognitive Science research.

▪ If courses attesting to my Experimental Psychology Practice/Mathematics/
  Experimental Programming skills are not in my BA Transcript (I took them
  additionally), which documents do I need to submit to prove my skills?

  For the Internship attesting your Experimental Psychology Practice skills, please
  submit a certificate of your research engagement in duration of at least 180 hours.
  For Mathematics courses, please submit a Transcript/ Exam Note/ Professor
  statement with your grade and ECTS. For your Experimental Programming skills,
  please submit a proof of these skills in the form of a Transcript/ Exam Note/
  Professor statement with your grade and ECTS, and otherwise in the form of a
  professor statement confirming your skills as demonstrated, e.g. as part of an
  internship or student job.

▪ Can courses in Statistics be accepted as confirmation of Mathematics skills?

  No, only courses in Linear Algebra and Calculus satisfy this requirement. Courses
  in Statistics cannot be accepted as a proof of your knowledge of Mathematics,
  since they do not provide an in-depth introduction in the topics of Advanced
  Mathematics.

▪ I do not have neither Experimental Psychology Practice nor Mathematics
  skills nor Experimental Programming skills – Can I still apply?

  No, applicants that lack skills in two of the three domains of Mathematics,
  Experimental Psychology practice, and Experimental Programming cannot be
  admitted to the program. Conditional admission offers to the program are also
  cannot be provided.

  It is hard to say: It will depend on several factors, e. g., on the Choice Modules

  (usually Friday-Saturday) from 9 to 17 or from 10 to 18.

                                  ADMISSION
▪ If I cannot come to Germany this year, is it possible to preserve my admission
  and use it in the next academic year?

  No, it is not possible. The reason behind this is the following: since the admission
  procedure requires ranking the applicants, next year you may not get the same
  place in the ranking, since the new applicants may have stronger qualifications
  compared to applicants with whom you were ranked this year.
  Therefore, in this case, admitting you in the next academic year based on the
  previous year's results will be discriminating against another applicant with
  stronger qualifications whose place you'll fill.
  Therefore, if you are rejecting your place this year, you'll need to apply again and
  go through the same selection process again next year.

▪ What does admission with an obligation (Bridge Module) mean? When
  should I complete the Bridge Module? Will the Bridge Module count into my
  Master’s Degree?

  There is no need (and no option) to complete the Bridge Module prior to the
  beginning of studies – you will be able to register for it after enrollment. Each
  bridge module will count as 6 ECTS into your MSc degree. You can complete
  only one bridge module (that is specified in your admission notification) and you
  should register for a bridge module only if your admission notification contains
  the obligation to do so.

▪ I received a rejection, but I cannot agree with the reasons for rejection. How
  can I submit an appeal?

  As it is stated in your rejection notice, you have the right to appeal. To do so,
  please forward your appeal to the address indicated in your notice.

  In case of further questions, please contact Mrs. Julia Schindler from the
  Advisory Office for Master's Application and Admission.

             MAKING THE MOST OF YOUR STUDY TIME



▪ I am interested in participating in research projects/internship at the UP.
  What are the main research foci? What should I do to apply?

  There are several different labs within the Humanities faculty; they mostly
  conduct psychology research (with such methods as EEG, eye-tracking, TMS,
  kinematics, virtual reality, etc.) and computation modelling. Links with other
  departments can be established on the initiative of students willing to do research
  in a particular area. There is a Baby lab in the building; the Sports Dept. also has
  interest in projects about Cognitive Science.

  The list of Cognitive Sciences Labs can be seen here:
  https://www.uni-potsdam.de/en/cognitive-sciences/science-research/labs.html
  The Potsdam Embodied Cognition Group (PECoG; https://www.uni-
  potsdam.de/pecog/index.html), currently has the following research foci:
  embodiment, action simulation in perception and comprehension, numerical
  cognition, action-language interaction, links between numbers and other domains
  (e.g., language, music), emotions, decision-making, scene perception and
  processing comics (Dr. Laubrock), human-robot interaction (Dr. Zhou). You can
  see more detailed info here:

  https://www.uni-potsdam.de/de/pecog/themen-fuer-bama-abschlussarbeiten.html

  Please note the topics are not "cut in stone", it’s a general overview, so if you have
  a specific research idea you'd like to work on, you can contact someone who has
  the closest research interests to your topic to discuss your ideas.

  To apply, please send a motivation letter together with your academic CV to the
